# Sources — The World Today, Wednesday 25 November 2026

This document is the research record behind this edition's front page.
Every story printed on the page was carried by at least two distinct
news outlets on the day (cross-feed confirmation), and every number
printed on the page is quoted exactly as the confirming outlets
reported it. Nothing on the page comes from a single source, and no
figure was rounded, converted, or estimated by the editors.

## How the edition was selected

The day's wire and RSS intake covered several hundred items. Stories
were clustered by event, and a cluster qualified for the page only
when two or more outlets from the master list below reported it
independently. Qualifying clusters were then ranked by consequence
and geographic spread: the three strongest became the feature
stories, the next four filled the Around the World grid, and the
figures in the By the Numbers strip were taken from the selected
stories themselves, never from a separate statistic hunt.

Master outlet list for this edition:
