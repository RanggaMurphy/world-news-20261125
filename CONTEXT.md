why the story matters. A briefing introduces no new fact about

## The boundary between dossier and briefing

- A dossier statement can be checked against the day's
  articles.
- A briefing statement explains; it would still be true if the
  day's story had gone the other way.
- Where a briefing touches the day's facts at all, it touches
  only facts the dossier already holds, and it says which
  dossier holds them.
- If a briefing and a dossier disagree, the dossier wins.
- A briefing may say what would change the story; it may not
  say what did change it after the edition closed. Updates
  belong to the next edition, and this repository's briefings
  were all written inside this edition's day.
