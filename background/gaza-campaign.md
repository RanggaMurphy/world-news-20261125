# Gaza's internal security, in brief

Background for feature 1 (the killing of Naeem Abu Nahel). The
dossier is stories/01-gaza-strike.md.

## What an internal security chief does

In Gaza's Hamas administration, the internal security chief is
responsible for policing inside the territory: informants,
dissent, and the protection of the movement's own apparatus.
It is a post that matters in wartime twice over, because the
holder knows both the organisation's structure and its
suspicions. Killing the holder is therefore read by both sides
as more than a personnel loss.

## Why turnover is the signal

Abu Nahel had been in the post for two months. Rapid turnover
in a security post during a campaign usually means one of two
things: the predecessor was killed or removed, or the
organisation is rotating holders to make targeting harder.
Either reading says the same thing about pressure. That is why
the record prints the tenure next to the death: the tenure is
the analysis, supplied by the facts themselves.

## How the page handles the toll

Seven Palestinians were reportedly killed in Tuesday's strikes,
the chief among them. The word "reportedly" is the carriers'
and the page keeps it: a toll from a war zone, in the first
cycle, is a report, and the headline's job is to carry the
killing that is confirmed (a named office-holder) while the
facts carry the toll at its reported strength.

## What would change the story

A named successor (continuity) or a long vacancy (disruption),
and any Hamas statement characterising the strike's effect on
its internal apparatus.
