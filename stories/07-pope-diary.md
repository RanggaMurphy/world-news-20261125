# Story 7 - The Pope's diary (Around the World 4)

**Headline as printed:** Pope Francis' diary reveals claims he
saw the devil three times

**Position:** Around the World, row 2, right cell. The grid's
closing story and the source of the numbers strip's third
figure.

**Carriers:** BBC News, PBS (two).

**Region:** Europe / Vatican.

**Panel label:** VATICAN.

## What happened

Published extracts from the late Pope Francis' diary record his
claim that he saw the devil three times. One of the visions, the
diary says, happened in the choir loft during Mass. The extracts
were published after the pontiff's death, and the story was the
day's most unusual by a distance: a pope recording, in his own
hand, an experience his church's language treats with great
care.

## The facts on record

- The diary claims three visions of the devil.
- One vision is placed in the choir loft during Mass.
- The extracts were published after Francis' death.
- Two outlets carried the story on the day.

## Why it closes the grid

Every edition tries to end its grid on the story a reader will
repeat at dinner, provided two outlets stand behind it. This
one also earns its place editorially: it is the only story in
the intake about belief rather than force, law or accident, and
a page of nothing but force, law and accident would misread the
day. Its figure, three, completes the strip with a number no
reader expects, which is what a third strip figure is for.

## How the headline was chosen

The headline keeps the word "claims" because the diary's
visions are claims, recorded by their maker and unverifiable by
anyone else. Dropping the word would upgrade a diary entry into
an event. The strip's label does the same work ("diary says"),
so the number three never travels without its status attached.

## What was not printed

The other two visions' settings: the record the page was built
from details the choir loft vision, and the page prints the one
it can source rather than summarising all three from partial
copy. The diary's publication history (who held it, who chose
the extracts) is in the linked reporting.

## Watch next

Whether the Vatican comments on the extracts, and whether
further diary material is published.
