# Story 1 - Gaza strike (feature 1)

**Headline as printed:** Israeli air strike kills Hamas
internal security chief in Gaza

**Position:** Feature 1, left column. The edition's lead.

**Carriers:** BBC, BBC News, Reuters (three; two BBC brands and
one independent wire).

**Region:** Middle East.

**Panel label:** GAZA.

## What happened

An Israeli air strike killed Naeem Abu Nahel, Hamas's internal
security chief in Gaza, who had been in the post for two months.
He was among seven Palestinians reportedly killed in Israeli
strikes on Tuesday. The appointment's short tenure is part of
the story: internal security leadership in Gaza has turned over
quickly under the pressure of Israel's continuing campaign.

## The facts on record

- The dead man is Naeem Abu Nahel, Hamas's internal security
  chief in Gaza.
- He had held the post for two months.
- Seven Palestinians were reportedly killed in Tuesday's
  strikes, Abu Nahel among them.
- Three outlets carried the story on the day.

## Why it leads

It is the day's most consequential development in Gaza, and the
feature order follows consequence: a named senior figure killed,
a toll attached, and a campaign whose direction the appointment
turnover helps a reader judge. No other story in the intake
combined a named principal, a number and a continuing war.

## How the headline was chosen

The headline is the carriers' framing: the strike, the office,
the place. The name stays in the facts rather than the headline
because the office is what makes the death news beyond Gaza;
a reader who knows no names still learns what was hit.

## What was not printed

The strike's location inside Gaza and the weapon used: not in
two-outlet copy at press time. The identities of the other six
reported dead: the wires named the chief and counted the rest,
and the page follows the wires exactly on that point.

## Watch next

Who succeeds Abu Nahel, and how long the successor lasts: the
turnover rate is the story's forward signal.
