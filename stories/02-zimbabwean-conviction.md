# Story 2 - The Zimbabwean conviction (feature 2)

**Headline as printed:** Zimbabwean convicted in South Africa
of trying to fight in Ukraine

**Position:** Feature 2, centre column.

**Carriers:** Al Jazeera, BBC, BBC News (three).

**Region:** Africa / Europe.

**Panel label:** UKRAINE.

## What happened

A South African court convicted a Zimbabwean man of trying to
fight in Ukraine for Russia. The case sits inside a wider story
the record states plainly: hundreds of people from different
African countries have been recruited to fight for Russia,
according to Ukraine, and Kyiv has raised the recruitment
repeatedly this year. The conviction is one of the clearest
court outcomes yet in that widening story.

## The facts on record

- The convicted man is Zimbabwean; the court is South African.
- The charge was trying to fight in Ukraine for Russia.
- Ukraine says hundreds of Africans have been recruited to
  fight for Russia.
- Three outlets carried the conviction on the day.

## Why it is a feature

A verdict is a decision, and features are reserved for
decisions, open tolls and reversals. This one also connects
three regions in a single line (Africa, South Africa's courts,
Ukraine's war), which is the kind of connection a world page
exists to show. It is the only court outcome in the day's
intake with that reach.

## How the headline was chosen

The headline names nationality, venue and intent in the
carriers' order. The man's name stays in the linked reporting:
he is a private individual in a recruitment pipeline, and the
story's weight is the pipeline, not the person.

## What was not printed

The sentence: the record the page was built from reports the
conviction, and a sentence figure in single-outlet copy would
not have been printed in any case. The recruitment route (who
recruited him, through which country) stays in the linked
reporting for the same reason.

## Watch next

Further prosecutions in South Africa and elsewhere on the
recruitment route, and any Russian or Ukrainian statement that
puts a firmer number on the recruitment.
